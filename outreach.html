OUTREACH EVENTS

Rocket Robotics pride ourselves in our outreach events based in serving our community and providing accessibility. Check out just a few of our events below! 👇

CODING CLASSES
📍Montgomery County Libraries📍

Rocket Robotics has been hosting six-week Java and Python courses at Montgomery County Public Libraries since December 2024. We have held over 8 courses in 2026 alone at 3 libraries, with plans to expand to 2 more. Our courses are free of charge and open to all people curious about coding. If you're interested, reach out and see when we'll be at your library!

GOVERNMENT ADVOCACY
📍Montgomery County📍

Rocket Robotics has been an outspoken advocate for more support for school-based FIRST teams. Through our efforts, we have secured $592k in funding for school based robotics for FY2027, as well as a stipend for teachers who coach FIRST teams. Rocket Robotics was also tasked by the board with the creation of a handbook for school based robotics teams to be distributed across MCPS!

STEM EXPERIENCE
📍Children's National Hospital📍

Rocket Robotics has frequently visited the Children's National Hospital in Washington, D.C. to bring robotics and STEM to patients. We bring LEGO SPIKE kits to teach basic engineering and coding principles, as well as demo our robot.

STEM NIGHTS
📍Middle/Elementary Schools📍

Rocket Robotics is a frequent presenter at local middle and elemntary school STEM nights. We use these opportunities to introduce students to FIRST and get them engaged with STEM!
